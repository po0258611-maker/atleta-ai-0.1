## 1. Executive Summary

Athleta AI is an international-grade web & mobile application designed for natural athletes, fitness enthusiasts, and personal trainers. It provides evidence-based Fullbody workout prescription, high-definition 3D ray-traced anatomical muscle engagement mapping, IIFYM flexible nutrition calculation (NutriFlux Engine), and real-time AI biomechanical consultation (KINETIX AI™).

---

## 2. Core Operational Modules

1. **Command Center (`overview`)**: Executive readiness dashboard, weekly tensile volume indicators, and active split summary.
2. **Fullbody Matrix (`workout_engine`)**: Multi-variable auto-regulated workout prescription generator (2-5 days/week split).
3. **NutriFlux Engine (`diet`)**: Metabolic rate and IIFYM macronutrient calculator.
4. **KINETIX AI™ (`ai_coach`)**: Senior Biomechanical Intelligence powered by Google Gemini 3.6 Flash.
5. **BioAtlas 3D (`exercise_library`)**: 3D ray-traced anatomical library with agonist/synergist muscle engagement highlights.
6. **BioProfile Studio (`assessment`)**: Multi-athlete biometric data and fatigue profiling.
7. **APEX Membership (`subscription`)**: Premium subscription tier (R$ 15,00/mês or R$ 120,00/ano).
